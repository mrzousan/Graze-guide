Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026

Graze Guide is a reference for tortoise keepers. You can use it without an account, and it does not collect your name, email, location or contacts.

What stays on your device

  • Your chosen filters, such as the plant type you last browsed, are saved in your browser so the app remembers them. They never leave your device, and clearing your browser data removes them.
  • To work offline, the app saves its own files on your device.

What other companies can see

  • Hosting. The app is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any website host, GitHub receives your IP address and basic browser details when you load the app. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Fonts. The app loads its typefaces from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when the fonts download. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Links. Source links open other websites, which have their own privacy policies.

What we don't do

  • No analytics, advertising or tracking tools.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information.

Planned features

When these features launch, this policy will be updated before they're available:

  • Photo ID (Premium). Photos you choose to identify will be sent to our plant identification provider, Kindwise (Plant.id), to name the plant. Only photos you submit are sent.
  • Subscriptions. Payments will be handled by Apple or Google. We won't see your card details.

Children

Graze Guide is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here with a new effective date. For questions, open an issue on the Graze Guide GitHub page.
